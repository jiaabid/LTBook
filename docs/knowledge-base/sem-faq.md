# SEM: Frequently Asked Questions

> **Fictional sample content.** This FAQ was written for the ToolBook demo. It is not real instrument documentation.

## How long should I book the SEM for?

A first session for a new sample type usually takes two hours, including pump-down, finding the region of interest and focusing. Routine imaging of a known sample takes about one hour. Book at least 15 minutes extra if you need EDX.

## Can I do EDX (element analysis) on the SEM?

Yes. The EDX detector is available for all trained users. Use an accelerating voltage of at least 15 kV so the electron beam can excite the characteristic X-ray lines, and a working distance of 10 mm, where the detector is aligned. Acquire spectra for at least 30 seconds live time. Coated samples show peaks from the coating (gold or carbon); remember this when interpreting the spectrum.

## What should I do if I see a vacuum error during my session?

Switch off the beam immediately, do not vent the chamber, and contact lab staff. Note the error code shown in the vacuum panel. Do not try to restart the pumps yourself.

## Can I leave a long acquisition running overnight?

No. The SEM must not run unattended. If you need long acquisitions, book an extended slot during working hours and stay with the instrument.

## Where do I save my images?

Save images to your personal folder on the network drive (S:\SEM\<your name>). The local disk of the microscope computer is cleaned every Friday.
